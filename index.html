Zakat Calculator UAE

Calculate zakat in AED with clearer nisab checks and cleaner yearly records.

Zakat Calculator UAE is a focused iPhone app for estimating zakat across cash, gold, silver, shares, savings, business assets, and liabilities. It is built for people who want an AED-first workflow, a straightforward result, and a reliable place to keep yearly records.

AED-first workflow

Use UAE-friendly amounts and compare gold or silver nisab in one place.

Clear results

See net wealth, nisab threshold, and zakat due with a simple breakdown.

Built for records

Save calculations, export them, and keep a cleaner yearly zakat trail.
